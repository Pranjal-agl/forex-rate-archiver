# Anomaly detector evaluation on injected faults

Generated by `forex-archiver evaluate` (deterministic; re-run after changing a detector). 120 synthetic markets x 3 correlated banks x 260 business days. The shared market path is a fat-tailed (t4) random walk with calm, normal and turbulent volatility regimes, average daily vol 0.44% calibrated from the archive. Each bank adds a fixed markup and iid timing noise of 0.35x vol, quoted to 2 dp. 6 independent faults per bank series, seed 7. Scored through the production `detect_frame` path.

Positives are the injected points: one per spike, and the first day of a level shift. Precision is flags on injected points divided by all flags. Recall is the share of injected points flagged.
